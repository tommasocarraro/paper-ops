## 5. Frequency guidance

- **Hard rule:** section 6 below, and anything marked "never" above.
- **Strong tendency:** the section moves and sentence patterns. Follow them most of the time; let content break them when it must.
- **Light preference:** individual word choices.

Litmus test: *would I have written this sentence in one of my own papers?* If it feels forced, pull back.

## 6. AI patterns to avoid

- Inflated verbs and adjectives: delve, leverage, showcase, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust, seamless, novel (unless the novelty is the stated contribution), comprehensive, intricate, notably
- "It is worth noting that", "It is important to note", "plays a crucial role", "in the realm of", "a growing body of work"
- Negate-then-correct constructions: "This is not X; it is Y", "not only X but also Y"
- Trailing participle phrases that add no claim: ", highlighting the importance of...", ", demonstrating the effectiveness of..."
- Three-item lists used for rhythm
- Significance claims the results do not show
- Every paragraph the same length, every sentence the same length
- Swapping synonyms for a defined term
